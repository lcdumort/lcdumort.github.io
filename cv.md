## Profile

Computational chemist with a PhD in Physics, specialized in machine-learning
interatomic potentials, reactive force-field development, high-throughput
quantum chemistry, and large-scale atomistic modeling. Combines an analytical
chemistry foundation with physics-level rigor.

## Experience

### Research Scientist · German Aerospace Center (DLR)

*Cologne, Germany · 2025 – Present*

- Led and delivered cross-functional computational chemistry and materials
  science projects in collaboration with industry partners.
- Engineered robust data pipelines using state-of-the-art molecular modeling
  methods for high-throughput surface detection, resulting in the identification
  and characterization of unknown stable high-index surfaces.

### Doctoral Researcher · Center for Molecular Modeling, Ghent University

*Ghent, Belgium · 2023 – 2025*

- Developed scalable empirical reactive force fields and machine-learning
  potentials through novel optimization frameworks, successfully modeling
  alumina and hydrated silicate ionic liquids with a 5–10× simulation speed-up.

### Research Engineer · IFP Energies Nouvelles

*Rueil-Malmaison, France · 2020 – 2023*

- Optimized ReaxFF reactive force-field parameters through the development and
  deployment of machine-learning algorithms, enabling accurate modeling of
  complex alumina systems in a catalytic context.
- Board member of the doctoral association; organized network events, study
  trips, and PhD onboarding.

### Research Intern · Institut Laue-Langevin & European Synchrotron Radiation Facility

*Grenoble, France · 2019*

- Advanced soft-matter studies by identifying water contents from molecular
  dynamics simulations through 3D imaging and machine-learning approaches,
  resulting in a first-author publication.
