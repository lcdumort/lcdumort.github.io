I am a computational chemist with a PhD in Physics, working at the intersection
of molecular simulation, machine learning, and scientific software. My work
spans machine-learning interatomic potentials, reactive force-field development,
high-throughput quantum chemistry, and large-scale atomistic modeling.

I currently work as a Research Scientist at the **German Aerospace Center (DLR)**
in Cologne, where I explore quantum computing for chemistry and materials
science. Before that, I developed machine-learning methods for ReaxFF
parameterization at **IFP Energies Nouvelles** and completed my PhD at the
**Center for Molecular Modeling, Ghent University**.
